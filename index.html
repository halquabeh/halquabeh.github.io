Background

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI, specializing in machine learning optimization and interpretability. My work spans state-space models, neural network optimization, and advancing AI robustness.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Machine Learning at MBZUAI and an MSc in Mechanical Engineering from the joint MIT–Masdar Institute program.
